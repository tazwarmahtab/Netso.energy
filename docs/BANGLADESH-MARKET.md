# Market Analysis: Bangladesh RESCO Solar

## Overview

Netso Energy operates in the Bangladesh market under the **Renewable Energy Service Company (RESCO)** model. This is a Build-Own-Operate (BOO) framework where Netso finances, installs, and operates solar systems on industrial/commercial rooftops, and the client pays for the electricity generated.

## Key Stakeholders

- **SREDA (Sustainable and Renewable Energy Development Authority)**: The national regulator. Netso must be registered as a RESCO with SREDA.
- **IDCOL (Infrastructure Development Company Limited)**: The primary government-backed financier for renewable energy projects in Bangladesh. Securing IDCOL financing is critical for scale.
- **Utilities (BPDB, DESCO, DPDC, etc.)**: These entities manage the grid and are involved in Net Metering agreements.

## Regulatory Environment

- **Net Metering Guidelines (2018)**: Allows consumers to export excess solar energy to the grid and offset their bills. This makes the RESCO model financially attractive for factories with varying loads.
- **Import Duties**: Changes in duties on solar panels, inverters, and batteries significantly impact project CAPEX.
- **RESCO Registration**: Mandatory registration with SREDA to operate as a service provider.

## Target Market

- **Industrial Rooftops**: RMG (Ready-Made Garment) factories are the prime target due to large rooftop areas and high daytime electricity demand.
- **Commercial Buildings**: Shopping malls and office complexes.

## Strategic Priorities

1. **IDCOL Financing**: Move from equity-funded pilots to debt-funded scale.
2. **SREDA Compliance**: Ensure all registrations are current.
3. **Deal Pipeline**: Close Deal 1 (e.g., Farzana Group) to prove the model and build a track-record.

## Competitive Advantages

- **Lower Tariffs**: Providing electricity at a rate lower than the national grid.
- **Zero CAPEX for Clients**: Netso handles all upfront costs.
- **Maintenance**: Netso handles all O&M (Operations & Maintenance).
