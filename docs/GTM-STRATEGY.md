# Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy: Netso.energy

## Core Objective
Launch the Netso.energy marketing website and gather 500+ qualified homeowner sign-ups for the waitlist within 30 days.

## Target Audience (Phase 1)
- **Solar Tech Adopters:** Homeowners who already have solar installations and batteries.
- **ROI Seekers:** Homeowners frustrated by the lack of clear financial data from their existing inverter apps.
- **Energy Independence Advocates:** Users looking to minimize grid reliance.

## Key Messaging
- **Primary Hook:** "The Energy Independence You Were Promised."
- **Problem:** "The Invisible Energy Avalanche" (Hidden costs and grid complexity).
- **Solution:** "Hardware-Agnostic Energy Intelligence."

## Marketing Channels

### 1. Organic Content (SEO & Education)
- **Glossary of Home Energy:** Simple, SEO-optimized articles explaining: Net Metering, EPR (energy specific), Battery Cycling, Inverter Efficiency.
- **"The True Cost" Series:** Blog posts/graphics showing how much homeowners actually save vs. what their inverter app tells them.

### 2. Community & Partnerships
- **Reddit & Forums:** Engage in `r/solar`, `r/homeautomation`, and `r/energystorage`.
- **Solar Installers:** Partner with local installers to offer Netso as a "Premium Software Layer" for their clients.
- **Facebook Groups:** Join "Solar Owners Australia/USA/UK" groups to gather feedback and build hype.

### 3. Paid (Optional - Beta Phase)
- **Search Ads:** Targeted keywords: "best solar monitoring app", "optimize home battery", "save on grid costs".
- **Retargeting:** Focus on visitors who explored the 3D storytelling journey but didn't sign up.

## The Waitlist Strategy
- **Exclusive Early Access:** Waitlist members get to beta test the app for free for the first 6 months.
- **Referral Loop:** "Refer a friend to the waitlist and move up 10 spots." (Increases viral coefficient).

## Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Waitlist Size:** 500+ within 30 days.
- **Conversion Rate (Visitors -> Signups):** > 5%.
- **Cost per Signup (CPA):** < $2.00 (if using paid).
- **Organic Traffic:** 2,000+ unique visitors in month 1.

## Timeline
- **Week 1:** Finalize marketing site & waitlist form.
- **Week 2:** Launch site & begin community outreach.
- **Week 3:** Content marketing (3 blog posts) & referral program launch.
- **Week 4:** Analysis of feedback & iteration on messaging.
