# Brand Identity: Netso.energy

## Brand Vision

To be the most trusted and intuitive platform for household energy independence. We empower homeowners to take control of their energy future with beauty and simplicity.

## Brand Values

- **Empowerment**: Giving users the tools to be independent.
- **Transparency**: Clear, real-time data on energy production and consumption.
- **Sustainability**: Promoting a cleaner, greener world through optimization.
- **Simplicity**: Complex energy management made intuitive.

## Color Palette

| Role      | Name          | Hex     | Usage                            |
|-----------|---------------|---------|----------------------------------|

| Secondary | Teal          | #00D4FF | Highlights, energy flows, CTAs   |
| Accent    | Amber         | #FF8C42 | Alerts, energy storage, data points|

## Typography

| Role     | Typeface      | Usage                            |
|----------|---------------|----------------------------------|
| Heading  | Geist         | Page titles, hero headers        |
| Body     | Inter         | Paragraphs, descriptions         |

## Tone of Voice

- **Direct**: We don't use jargon. We explain things simply.
- **Optimistic**: Energy independence is achievable and exciting.
- **Informative**: We provide value through insights, not just data.
- **Supportive**: We are a partner in the user's energy journey.
