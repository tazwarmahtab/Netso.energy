# Netso Intelligence Knowledge Base

This document serves as the central repository for all verified information regarding Netso Energy, its mission, business model, and technical infrastructure, extracted from local and company-wide sources.

## Executive Summary
Netso Energy is Bangladesh's first rooftop solar infrastructure network, operating as a **Renewable Energy Service Company (RESCO)**. Our mission is to achieve energy independence for every household through distributed rooftop solar infrastructure.

## 1. Business Model: The RESCO Advantage
Unlike traditional EPC contractors, Netso utilizes a **Build-Own-Operate (BOO)** framework.
- **Zero-Upfront Cost:** Building owners pay nothing for installation or equipment.
- **Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs):** Customers pay Netso for solar electricity at tariffs lower than the national grid.
- **Net Metering:** Excess energy is exported to the national grid (BPDB/DESCO) via net metering, providing passive income for building owners.
- **Long-term Ownership:** Netso owns, operates, and maintains the infrastructure for the duration of the PPA (typically 15-20 years).

## 2. Core Pillars & Products
- **Solar Pergola System:** Our flagship branded product. Elevated architectural solar structures that generate power while creating premium, usable shaded space on rooftops.
- **AI Energy Optimization:** A suite of tools for real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and generation forecasting across the entire fleet.
- **Solar Scoring Engine (SVS):** A prospecting tool that computes the **Solar Viability Score** for buildings using Google Solar API data and local irradiance fallbacks for Dhaka.

## 3. Technical Infrastructure
- **Mobile Platform:** React Native (Expo) app for monitoring generation, consumption, and savings.
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express with MongoDB Atlas.
- **IoT Integration:** Real-time inverter data via MQTT broker (supporting Deye/Growatt).
- **Inverter Communication:** Automated push of live packets to `POST /api/inverter/push`.

## 4. Market & Regulatory Context (Bangladesh)
- **Regulators:** SREDA (RESCO registration) and IDCOL (primary financing partner).
- **Utility Partners:** BPDB, DESCO, DPDC for net metering integration.
- **Target Market:** Industrial RMG factories (e.g., Farzana Group) and commercial rooftops in urban Bangladesh.
- **Key Project:** **Deal 1** with Farzana Group factory is the current priority pilot.

## 5. Strategic Identity
- **Tagline:** "Rooftops that power cities."
- **Brand Colors:** Abyss, Solar Orange, and Aqua.
- **Vision:** Energy independence for every household.
