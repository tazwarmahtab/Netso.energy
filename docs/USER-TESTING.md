# User Testing: Netso.energy Marketing Website

## Test Objectives

- **Narrative Resonance:** Does the "Energy Independence" and "Invisible Energy Avalanche" story resonate with homeowners?
- **Clarity of Value Prop:** Do users understand what Netso does and why it's better than their default inverter app?
- **Conversion Friction:** Is the waitlist sign-up form intuitive and easy to complete?
- **3D Performance:** Does the 3D scroll-driven narrative feel smooth and engaging, or is it distracting/slow?

## User Personas

- **Persona 1: Solar Sam (Tech-Savvy Early Adopter)**
  - **Role:** Data-focused homeowner who installed solar panels and a battery 2 years ago.
  - **Technical Level:** High (uses smart home apps, checks inverter daily).
  - **Key Goals:** Maximize ROI, see high-granularity data, optimize battery cycling.

- **Persona 2: Eco-Conscious Emma (Values-Driven Homeowner)**
  - **Role:** Homeowner who cares about sustainability and is new to solar.
  - **Technical Level:** Low to Medium.
  - **Key Goals:** Feel good about energy production, understand environmental impact, ensure system is working correctly.
